Shopify Store Design / Beauty & Self-Care

Liora Care

A beauty and self-care e-commerce concept designed around simplicity, warmth, and an elevated shopping experience.

Project Overview

The goal was to create a digital storefront that reflects the warmth and elegance of a premium beauty brand, while ensuring a frictionless, intuitive shopping experience.

Live Store lilit-harutyunyan-0chbyehc.myshopify.com ↗
My Role UI/UX Design, Shopify Theme Customization
Technologies Shopify, Liquid, HTML, CSS, JavaScript

Creative Direction

The visual language is built on a warm beige and milky color palette, paired with elegant typography. The layout prioritizes lifestyle-focused content and brand storytelling, using generous whitespace to let the products breathe.

Homepage & Collection Layouts

The homepage guides visitors through a curated journey: from the hero brand statement to featured collections, and finally to lifestyle imagery that builds trust.

Mobile Responsive Design

Every interaction is optimized for touch. The navigation collapses into an elegant full-screen menu, and product galleries become swipeable carousels for a seamless mobile shopping experience.

Outcome & Reflection

This project deepened my understanding of e-commerce UX patterns and the importance of visual hierarchy in driving user action. By focusing on a cohesive design system and custom Liquid sections, the storefront feels premium, trustworthy, and easy to navigate across all devices.
